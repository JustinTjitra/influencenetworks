# Event dates and timing: Indonesian political and policy events, 2024-2025

**How to read these notes (applies to every fact below).**
- **No page was read.** The single permitted fetch attempt (ekonomi.bisnis.com) failed with a DNS/allow-list error and was not retried. Everything rests on web-search output. 25 of 25 permitted searches were used.
- Evidence tags used per fact:
  - **[T]** = the article title and URL appeared in the search results (directly observed).
  - **[S]** = statement taken from the search tool's summary of the result pages (one step less direct than a snippet; treat as unconfirmed until the page is opened).
  - **[U]** = date/time read from the URL itself. CNN Indonesia and CNBC Indonesia URLs embed `YYYYMMDDHHMMSS`; Bisnis URLs embed `YYYYMMDD`; Jawa Pos IDs begin `YYMMDD`. Katadata article IDs begin with 8 hex characters that decode to a Unix time (decoded to WIB here; this is an undocumented convention, internally consistent across 9 articles checked, so treat as an inference, not a fact).
- Weekdays were computed with `date`, not taken from sources.
- Per the study's rule, nothing about share prices, index moves or market reaction was searched for or recorded.
- All times are WIB (UTC+7). "Close" means the IDX regular-market close at about 16:00 WIB (see last section).
