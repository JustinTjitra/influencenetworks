## 1. Golkar ministers in Jokowi's Onward Indonesia Cabinet (2019-10-23 to 2024-10-20)

### Takeaway
Golkar held minister posts for the whole term: Airlangga Hartarto (Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs) and Agus Gumiwang Kartasasmita (Minister of Industry) were appointed on 2019-10-23 and both carried on into Prabowo's cabinet in October 2024. The third Golkar seat (Youth and Sports) passed from Zainudin Amali to Dito Ariotedjo on 2023-04-03, and Bahlil Lahadalia became Energy Minister on 2024-08-19.

## 4. Aburizal Bakrie's positions in Golkar

### Takeaway
Aburizal Bakrie ceased to be Golkar general chairman on 2016-05-17, when the extraordinary congress (Munaslub) in Nusa Dua, Bali elected Setya Novanto and named Bakrie chair of the Dewan Pembina. He was confirmed again as Dewan Pembina chair on 2019-12-05 for 2019-2024, lost that post when Agus Gumiwang Kartasasmita was named to it on 2024-08-22, and was named chair of the Dewan Kehormatan (honorary council) for 2024-2029 on 2024-11-07.
