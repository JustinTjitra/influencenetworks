## No page could be opened, so every fact here is snippet-grade

All five research strands ran under the same constraint; the bank strand records its research date as 2026-11-20. Page fetching is blocked in the research environment: each strand made one attempt (on www.idx.co.id, www.smart-tbk.com, en.wikipedia.org, ekonomi.bisnis.com and api.crossref.org respectively), each attempt failed (with a DNS or allow-list error where the notes state the cause), and none was retried. The search budgets (20 searches for banks, 30 for palm oil, media and exchange access, 25 each for cabinet facts, event dates and literature) were used in full. Consequently **nothing below was read on the page**: no annual report was opened, no news article was read, no publisher or DOI landing page was resolved, and no link is confirmed to be live, complete or final. Every URL is reproduced exactly as the notes give it, and the notes state that each one was copied from a search result and none was built from a pattern.

The notes' own evidence labels are kept. "Not found" means a URL did not appear in a search result, not that the document does not exist. "Probable, unverified" marks a link whose year rests on a snippet or an ambiguous file name. In the cabinet section, **[S-quote]** means the search summary reproduced wording attributed to the cited page, **[S-sum]** means the summary stated the fact without quoted wording, **[title]** means only the headline or URL slug supports it, and **UNVERIFIED** means no search result confirmed it. In the events section, **[T]** means the article title and URL appeared in results, **[S]** means the statement comes from the search tool's summary, and **[U]** means the date or time was read from the URL itself (CNN Indonesia and CNBC Indonesia URLs embed `YYYYMMDDHHMMSS`; Bisnis URLs embed `YYYYMMDD`; Jawa Pos IDs begin `YYMMDD`; Katadata article IDs are decoded from eight hex characters, an undocumented convention that the notes treat as an inference, not a fact). Weekdays were computed, not taken from sources. In the literature section, "VERIFIED" never means the publisher page was read: **VERIFIED-HANDLE** means volume, issue, year and pages are encoded in a RePEc handle URL, **VERIFIED-SNIPPET** means a bibliographic record's snippet stated them, **QUERY-ECHO RISK** means the researcher supplied the detail in the query and the summary repeated it, **PARTLY VERIFIED** means title and journal only, and **FROM MEMORY, NOT VERIFIED** means no search result confirmed it. Following the study's rule, this report describes each cited paper's design and non-price findings only and leaves out return estimates.

### The exchange's filings page is the fallback, chosen by on-page filters

The Indonesia Stock Exchange has a "Laporan Keuangan dan Tahunan" page under its listed-companies section ([idx.co.id](https://www.idx.co.id/id/perusahaan-tercatat/laporan-keuangan-dan-tahunan)) with an English twin, "Financial Statements & Annual Report" ([idx.co.id](https://www.idx.co.id/en/listed-companies/financial-statements-and-annual-report)). Per the search summary, it lists filings per issuer code with period filters "Triwulan 1", "Triwulan 2", "Triwulan 3" and "Tahunan", and each company typically has PDF, Excel and XBRL files. **The page address is fixed: no per-company URL appeared in any result**, so company and year are chosen on the page. Filed annual reports are stored under an announcements path with a year-month folder and a hashed file name, as in one listed company's "Laporan Tahunan 2024 / Annual Report 2024" filed in April 2025 ([idx.co.id StaticData example](https://www.idx.co.id/StaticData/NewsAndAnnouncement/ANNOUNCEMENTSTOCK/From_EREP/202504/76c017266a_153ef21e33.pdf)), which means exchange-hosted files cannot be predicted from the ticker. A different page under "Tentang BEI" holds the exchange's own corporate annual reports and is not the one wanted ([idx.co.id tentang-bei](https://www.idx.co.id/id/tentang-bei/laporan-tahunan/)). The filing duty itself comes from OJK Regulation 29/POJK.04/2016, with financial statements filed in XBRL under the exchange's disclosure rule Kep-00015/BEI/01-2021 ([idx.co.id Peraturan I-E](https://www.idx.co.id/media/9622/peraturan_i_e_kewajiban_penyampaian_informasi.pdf)).

This page is the workable route for the reports with no direct link, starting with Mandiri, BCA and BTN for 2025 and BNI for 2024 (issuer codes BMRI, BBCA, BBTN, BBNI, period "Tahunan"). Three things about it remain unknown because the exchange's domain was unreachable: whether the page accepts the issuer code or year as an address parameter, whether the "Tahunan" filter returns the annual report itself or only the audited annual financial statements (the summary's mention of PDF, Excel and XBRL fits financial statements, so the annual report may sit under a separate selector), and whether scripted downloads are allowed. No exchange-hosted link for any of the twelve banks' 2024 or 2025 annual reports appeared in results, and the palm oil and media searches returned none either.

The notes add three leads from the researcher's background knowledge, each explicitly not verified in the session and to be treated as leads, not findings. First, the request form `/primary/ListedCompany/GetCompanyProfilesDetail?KodeEmiten=XXXX&language=id-id`, returning a JSON object whose top-level keys include `Profiles`, `Sekretaris`, `Direktur`, `Komisaris`, `KomiteAudit`, `PemegangSaham`, `AnakPerusahaan`, `KAP` and `Dividen`, with `Profiles` carrying fields such as `TanggalPencatatan` (listing date): check these names against the nichsedge/idx-bei source. Third, that the endpoint is an undocumented internal API used by the site's front end, which the pattern of independent scrapers and absent documentation supports. Whether the endpoint currently responds without a browser session was not tested, and no terms of use for it were found.

## Conclusion

The research turns the project's next steps into a download-and-confirm job for the owner's machine. Because the research environment could not open a single page, the right way to use this report is as a checklist: each link is a lead with a named check (cover year, language, annual report versus sustainability report or financial statements), and each date is a claim with a named weaker point (a URL timestamp, a decoded article ID, a search summary). The firms most likely to cost time are those with no direct link in either year (BWPT, BMTR, MNCN, MSIN, VIVA, TMPO, MARI), those whose links are ambiguous (BBRI 2025, SIMP and LSIP 2025, SCMA, ANJT 2025, SSMS 2024) and the three large banks whose 2025 report surfaced only on an archive page.

Three findings change how rows should be entered.
